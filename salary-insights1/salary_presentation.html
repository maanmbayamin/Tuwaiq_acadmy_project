01 · The question

How much does a data or AI job pay, and why?

Our target is salary in USD per year. We used two datasets: one big global file, and a small Saudi file to fill a gap.

Global salaries

13,972 records from 2020 to 2024. Each row has the job title, experience level, country, work setting, company size and salary.

Most records come from US companies, so a "global" average mostly describes the US market.

Saudi benchmark

The global file has only 2 rows from Saudi Arabia. Too few to say anything. So we collected 11 salary ranges from recruiters and salary websites.

Robert Walters 6 roles Levels.fyi Glassdoor Paylab ZenHR PayScale

These are monthly SAR values. We converted them with the fixed rate 1 USD = 3.75 SAR, and used them for comparison only, not for training.

Why not more accurate?

The data has no skills, company name, city or years of experience. Two people with the same profile can earn very different amounts, so the app always shows a likely range. The real salary falls inside it about 8 times out of 10.

07 · Takeaways

What to remember

Experience and country lead

An executive earns about 2.3x an entry-level employee. A US company pays about 5x an Indian company for similar work.

ML, AI and research pay most

Around $175K median, against about $109K for analytics and BI roles.

Remote does not mean less

On-site and fully remote jobs have almost the same median salary.

Saudi: two stories

Analysts and data scientists earn below the US but close to Europe. Senior data leaders are paid above the global median.

Estimates are ranges

The model gives a typical salary and a likely range. Useful to start a conversation, not to replace an offer.

Limits

The data is 87% US, Saudi numbers come from outside sources, and skills or company details are missing.
